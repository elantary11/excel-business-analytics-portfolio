# Excel Business Analytics Dashboard Portfolio

This portfolio brings together three Excel dashboards from very different domains: video games, airline bookings, and real estate. Each one starts with a different business question, but follows the same process of cleaning the data, defining useful KPIs, and building an interactive view for decision-making.

## 1. GameScope: Sales and Trends

![GameScope dashboard](assets/gamescope.png)

An analysis of historical video-game performance across platforms, genres, regions, and time.

**Highlighted metrics**

- $8.92B in global sales
- 16.72K games
- Wii Sports as the leading title at approximately $82.5M
- Action as the leading genre at approximately $1.75B
- PS2 as the leading platform at approximately $1.26B

The dashboard also compares regional sales by genre and visualizes the historical rise and decline of global game sales. These views can support portfolio planning by showing where past consumer demand concentrated, while recognizing that historical leadership alone does not predict future demand.

## 2. Booking Insights

![Booking insights dashboard](assets/booking-insights.png)

An operational booking dashboard focused on channel behavior, lead time, completion, cancellation, add-ons, and booking patterns by day and hour.

**Highlighted metrics**

- 44K bookings
- 85-day average lead time
- 84% cancelled and 16% completed in the supplied report
- Channel, trip-type, baggage, and meal filters
- 92% of bookings through the Internet channel in the displayed selection
- 67.8% baggage add-on adoption and 43% meal add-on adoption

The heatmap identifies booking volume by day and hour, allowing campaign timing and capacity planning to be explored. The unusually high cancellation share is an important analytical finding that should be investigated through data-definition and source-quality checks before making operational recommendations.

## 3. Real Estate Overview

![Real estate dashboard](assets/real-estate.png)

A property portfolio view comparing sales, availability, location, property type, and furnishing status.

**Highlighted metrics**

- 106 properties
- $126.2M in total sales
- $2.69M average property price
- Cairo contributes 45% of the sales distribution shown in the dashboard
- 55 properties are available versus 51 sold
- City-level comparisons across Cairo, Alexandria, and Giza

The available inventory represents 55% of the portfolio shown. This gives sales and marketing teams a clear inventory target, but the next analytical step should calculate the monetary value and aging of unsold properties rather than relying on unit counts alone.
